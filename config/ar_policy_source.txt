Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy (Demo)

This policy defines when an employee travel expense claim is reimbursable.
All amounts are in US dollars.

1. An expense is reimbursable only if the receipt is submitted within 30 days after the
   expense date. If the receipt is submitted more than 30 days after the expense date,
   the expense is not reimbursable.

2. A meal expense is reimbursable only if the meal amount is at most 75 dollars per day.
   If a meal amount is greater than 75 dollars per day, the meal expense is not reimbursable.

3. A hotel expense is reimbursable only if the room rate is at most 250 dollars per night.
   If the room rate is greater than 250 dollars per night, the hotel expense is not
   reimbursable.

4. Economy class airfare is reimbursable.

5. Business class airfare is reimbursable only if the flight duration is greater than
   8 hours. If the flight duration is at most 8 hours, business class airfare is not
   reimbursable.

6. Alcohol is never reimbursable, regardless of the amount or the submission date.

7. A claim requires manager approval if the total claim amount is greater than
   1000 dollars. A claim with a total amount of at most 1000 dollars does not require
   manager approval.

8. Only full-time employees and part-time employees are eligible to submit an expense
   claim. Contractors are not eligible to submit an expense claim.
